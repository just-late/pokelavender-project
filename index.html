pokélavender project

Pokémon Lavender is a fan-made rom hack
of Pokémon Crystal Version based on the Polished Crystal engine
by Rangi42.

It features a brand new story set in the pokémon world
that takes place in the atuva region.
It is currently unreleased, but a demo is in the works.
